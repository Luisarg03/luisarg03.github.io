## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Gradient bars between major sections
Major section boundaries SHALL be separated by a thin gradient bar (accent to transparent) spanning the full content width.

#### Scenario: Gradient bar visible between sections
- **WHEN** two major sections meet
- **THEN** a 2px gradient bar separates them visually

### Requirement: Optional vertical connector line
A subtle vertical connector line MAY run along the left edge of the content area, linking all sections from Hero through Contact.

#### Scenario: Connector present on desktop
- **WHEN** viewport width is ≥ 1024px
- **THEN** a thin vertical line is visible on the left edge connecting sections
